HexFuse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app HexFuse (package com.insoftyx.hexfuse)

HexFuse is a hexagon puzzle game by Insoftyx ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have. You can play without creating an account, and we do not ask for your email address, phone number or real name.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress (best score, levels and stars, achievements, settings, the run in progress, the name shown on the leaderboard, purchase status) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent anywhere except the parts listed below. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it.

2. Information the game collects and shares

FeatureWhat is usedWhy
Leaderboards (Global and Country) A random anonymous player ID (Firebase Authentication, no sign-in needed), the name you show on the board, your country, your score, and details of the run that made it (colour level-ups and biggest dice), for all time, the week and the day. To show the top players and your own rank.
Google Play Games If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, your Play Games display name and your score are used for the leaderboard. We do not receive your Google account email. To show your name on the leaderboard and keep your best score.
Your country The country is worked out on your device from your SIM card, mobile network or device region setting. It is not your precise location, and the game never asks for location permission. To place you on the Country board.
Ads (Google AdMob) Your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, collected by Google. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choice (see section 4). To show rewarded ads (for example to continue a run, get a free Undo or Shuffle, re-roll a draft or double the daily gift), a banner on the home screen and ads between runs, and to measure them.
Analytics (Firebase Analytics) An app instance ID, device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language, approximate region from IP address) and gameplay events such as screens opened, runs started and finished, level reached, ads shown, and settings changed. To understand how the game is used and improve its balance and quality.
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) Crash and error details, device model, operating system, and the game mode, stage and screen at the time of the crash. To find and fix crashes.
Purchases (Google Play Billing) Whether you bought "Remove ads" or the "Premium Pack". The payment itself is handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To remove ads and restore your purchase.

Leaderboard information is public: your name, country and score can be seen by other players. Do not use a name that contains personal information.

3. Who receives information

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore database, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Play Games, Google Play Billing). Google processes this data under its own terms. See policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Advertising partners that take part in Google's ad network, for the ads you see.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for anything other than the purposes above.

4. Your choices

  • Ads consent. In some regions (for example the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent before showing ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad personalisation choices in the game.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset it or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Name on the leaderboard. If you are not signed in to Google Play Games you can change your name on the Leaderboard screen at any time. A Play Games name is the one in your Play Games profile.
  • Remove ads and Premium Pack. "Remove ads" is a one-time purchase that removes the banner and the ads shown between runs. The "Premium Pack" is a one-time purchase that also removes them and adds a starting bonus to each Endless run. Rewarded ads you choose to watch stay optional.

5. Retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept until they are deleted. Analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase for a limited time, set in our Firebase project (between 2 and 14 months for analytics events). To have your leaderboard entry or other data we hold about you deleted, email us at the address below and tell us the name and country shown on your entry (and, if you can, your Play Games name); we will remove it. Uninstalling the game removes the data on your device and its anonymous player ID, which makes your old entry unreachable by you.

6. Children

HexFuse is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is sent to Google services over encrypted connections. Our database rules allow a player to write only their own leaderboard entries. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Changes

If we change what the game collects or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top. The version of the game you have may link to the current page.
